# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project. Participants must communicate constructively, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy and attribution, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, or disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s GitHub contact channel. Maintainers will review reports fairly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and take proportionate corrective action.
