# Data licences and safety agreement

Before protected preview/download, users must accept both the current data licence snapshot and the versioned Store safety/no-liability statement. Acceptance is bound to user, DataTiles SHA-256, rights fingerprint, and safety-agreement version.

The Store records acceptance as evidence of assent. It does not relicense upstream data, waive non-waivable law, certify accuracy, or make a product suitable for navigation. A changed file, rights statement, or safety version requires fresh acceptance.
